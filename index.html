About Me

I'm a Software Engineer who enjoys building things that actually work well and look good doing it. I graduated with a BS in Information Technology, specializing in Web and Mobile Development, from Bulacan State University. Since then, I've been focused on writing clean code, learning scalable patterns, and growing as a developer every day.
